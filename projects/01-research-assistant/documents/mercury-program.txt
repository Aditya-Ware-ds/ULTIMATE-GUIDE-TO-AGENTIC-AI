Mercury Program (1958-1963)

Project Mercury was the United States' first human spaceflight program. Its
goal was to put a human in orbit around Earth and return them safely. Alan
Shepard became the first American in space on May 5, 1961, with a suborbital
flight. John Glenn became the first American to orbit Earth on February 20,
1962, aboard Friendship 7, completing three orbits. The program ran a total of
six crewed missions between 1961 and 1963.
