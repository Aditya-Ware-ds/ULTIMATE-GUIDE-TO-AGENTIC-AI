Space Shuttle Program (1981-2011)

The Space Shuttle was the first reusable crewed spacecraft, flown by NASA from
1981 to 2011. The first flight, STS-1, launched on April 12, 1981, with
Columbia. The fleet included five orbiters: Columbia, Challenger, Discovery,
Atlantis, and Endeavour. The program suffered two fatal accidents: Challenger
disintegrated shortly after launch on January 28, 1986, and Columbia
disintegrated during reentry on February 1, 2003. The Shuttle was used to
build the International Space Station starting in 1998.
