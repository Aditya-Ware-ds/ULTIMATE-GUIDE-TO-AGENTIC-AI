Apollo Program (1961-1972)

The Apollo program followed Mercury and Gemini, with the goal of landing
humans on the Moon. Apollo 11 achieved this on July 20, 1969, when Neil
Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walked on the lunar surface while Michael Collins
orbited above in the command module. Five more crewed landings followed
through Apollo 17 in December 1972. Apollo 13, launched in April 1970, suffered
an oxygen tank failure en route to the Moon and became a mission focused on
safely returning the crew, which succeeded.
