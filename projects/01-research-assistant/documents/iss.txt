International Space Station (1998-present)

The International Space Station (ISS) is a modular space station in low Earth
orbit, a collaboration between NASA, Roscosmos, ESA, JAXA, and CSA. The first
module, Zarya, launched in November 1998. The station has been continuously
crewed since November 2000. It orbits Earth roughly every 90 minutes at an
altitude of about 400 kilometers. The Space Shuttle program was the primary
vehicle used to deliver and assemble most of its major modules.
