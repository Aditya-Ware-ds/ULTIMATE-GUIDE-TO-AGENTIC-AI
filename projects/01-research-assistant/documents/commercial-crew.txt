Commercial Crew Program (2011-present)

After the Space Shuttle retired in 2011, NASA relied on Russian Soyuz
spacecraft to reach the ISS until the Commercial Crew Program produced new
options. SpaceX's Crew Dragon flew its first crewed mission, Demo-2, in May
2020, restoring American crewed launch capability from U.S. soil. Boeing's
Starliner, the program's second contracted vehicle, flew its first crewed test
flight in 2024. The program marked a shift toward NASA purchasing transport
services from commercial providers rather than owning the spacecraft itself.
