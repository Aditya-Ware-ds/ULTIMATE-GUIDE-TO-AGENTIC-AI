# Module 21 resources

Verified 2026-09-22.

- [Hugging Face TRL: GRPO Trainer](https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/main/en/grpo_trainer) -- the current, verified source for GRPO's exact group-relative advantage formula and its no-critic-model design, used directly in lesson 03 and this module's lab.
- [DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03300) -- the paper that introduced GRPO, confirmed real via its arXiv abstract.
- [Anthropic: Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents) -- also referenced in Modules 04/08/12/17/19/20; its evaluation-first framing underlies why RLVR's verifiability requirement matters as much for training as Module 16's mechanical-check preference does for evaluation.
