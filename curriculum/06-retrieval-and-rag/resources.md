# Module 06 resources

Verified 2026-09-22.

- [OpenAI Platform Docs: Embeddings](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/embeddings) -- current `text-embedding-3-small`/`-large` usage and pricing referenced in lesson 01.
- [Voyage AI: Embeddings documentation](https://docs.voyageai.com/docs/embeddings) -- an alternative current embedding provider referenced in lesson 01.
- [Pinecone Learn: Hybrid Search](https://www.pinecone.io/learn/hybrid-search-intro/) -- vendor-neutral explanation of combining sparse (lexical) and dense (semantic) search, behind lesson 02.
- [Okapi BM25 -- Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okapi_BM25) -- the standard lexical scoring algorithm referenced (but not implemented) in lesson 02; this module's lab uses a simpler overlap score for teaching clarity.
- [Anthropic: Contextual Retrieval](https://www.anthropic.com/news/contextual-retrieval) -- primary-source discussion of chunking and retrieval quality trade-offs referenced in lesson 01/02.
