# Module 16 resources

Verified 2026-09-22.

- [SWE-bench](https://www.swebench.com/) -- official leaderboards and benchmark family (SWE-bench Verified, Multilingual, Multimodal, Lite), confirmed actively maintained.
- [GAIA benchmark (Hugging Face)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gaia-benchmark/GAIA) -- 450+ real-world questions across three difficulty levels, confirmed actively maintained with a 2025-10 dataset format update.
- [Anthropic: Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents) -- also referenced in Modules 04/08; its evaluation framing (test against realistic scenarios, not synthetic ones) applies directly to this module's golden-dataset lesson.
- [G-Eval: NLG Evaluation using GPT-4 with Better Human Alignment (Liu et al., 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.16634) -- foundational paper behind the LLM-as-judge pattern in lesson 02.
- [Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena (Zheng et al., 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685) -- the paper that documented position, verbosity, and self-preference bias in LLM judges, referenced in lesson 02.
